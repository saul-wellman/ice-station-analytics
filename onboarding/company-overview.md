# Company Overview: Ice Station

## Who we are

Ice Station is a fictional mid-sized omnichannel retailer selling home, office, and everyday lifestyle products through physical stores and an online channel.

## Business footprint

- Headquarters: Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh
- Initial operating regions: Central India
- Currency: Indian rupees (INR)
- Reporting calendar: calendar year
- Initial channels: Store and Online
- Initial departments: Sales, Finance, Operations, Marketing, Customer Support

All company details and data are invented for practice.

## Business model

Customers place orders containing one or more products. Orders may be paid using UPI, card, net banking, cash, or wallet. Orders can be completed, cancelled, or returned. Store teams and the e-commerce team report sales performance to regional managers.

## Your role

You are a newly onboarded Junior Data Analyst. Your responsibilities are to:
- answer business questions using data;
- investigate data-quality issues;
- write reusable SQL queries and views;
- validate figures against documented definitions;
- build Power BI reports and explain findings in plain language;
- document assumptions and communicate limitations.

## People you may hear from

- Head of Sales: sales trends, stores, channels, and product performance
- Finance Manager: revenue definitions, reconciliations, and payment totals
- Operations Lead: fulfilment, returns, and store comparisons
- Marketing Manager: campaign performance and customer acquisition
- Analytics Lead: data quality, KPI consistency, and report governance
